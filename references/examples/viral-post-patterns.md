# Viral post patterns (study, 2026-10-20)

Source: linkedin-scout (Haiku, read only) ran 6 LinkedIn content searches for the past month: AI automation, AI agents, Claude Code, career switch AI, automated my job, AI India jobs. 29 posts were read. No post text was copied. Only structure notes are kept here.

**Caveat:** this is a small sample. Only 4 posts had 100+ reactions, and some counts were unclear on the search page. Treat these as starting patterns and check them against Faizan's own results after 4 to 6 weeks.

## Top posts (structure only)

| Reactions / comments | Author type | Format | Structure | Why it worked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 981 / 24 (92 reposts) | Developer advocate | Useful how to | Hook, plain explainer of a Claude Code skill, 5 point list, link, follow line | Useful list, people saved and reposted it |
| 659 / 168 | Career creator, India | News hook + take | India fresher hiring news, 2 short context paragraphs, 1 tip, question, hashtags | Big news for his audience plus an easy question |
| 374 / 35 | Founding engineer | Personal build + numbers | Annoying personal problem, fixed with Claude Code, result in numbers, short close | Relatable, specific, very short |
| 102 / 20 | Edtech page | Course plug | Bold hook, emoji skill list, article link | Topic demand (service company to AI roles), not the style |
| 25 / 34 | Founder | Story with a turn | His boss tried to automate his job, what that takes, question | More comments than reactions: the story invited people's own experience |
| 13 / 12 | Ops consultant | Story + question | Rogue agent story, definition, his view, question | Same: a question people could answer |
| 12 / 5 | Automation consultant | Short principle | Car analogy, one rule, close | Short and easy to agree with |

Short quote noted for style, with credit: Rishabh Chopra, "Automate friction, not accountability." (inspiration only, do not reuse)

## Takeaways
1. Personal and specific beats general. The engineer's small personal fix outperformed every company explainer.
2. A question that people can answer from their own lives drives comments.
3. Saves and reposts come from practical how to content.
4. India career news gets huge comment counts with an India audience. That is good for Faizan's India job goal.
5. Many high reach posts use bait, follow lines, emoji lists and links. Faizan does not use these. Building trust with recruiters matters more than raw reach.
