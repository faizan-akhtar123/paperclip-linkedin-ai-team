# Intentional engagement (base post for the engagement rules)

Saved 2026-10-22. Faizan chose this LinkedIn post as the basis of his engagement strategy. It is for inspiration and rules only; never copy its lines into his posts or comments.

## The post (author not recorded)
> If you can give LinkedIn 60mins a day, here's how I'd use that time to attract potential clients.
>
> → Start 5 conversations with potential buyers = 1,825 a year
> → Leave 5 useful comments on relevant posts = 1,825 a year
> → Follow up with 3 qualified prospects = 1,095 a year
> → Note down 1 real customer problem = 365 content ideas a year
>
> Now, here's where most founders get it wrong.
>
> They spend that hour:
>
> → Commenting on random viral posts.
> → Connecting with anyone who has "CEO" in their headline.
> → Posting content that attracts everyone except buyers.
> → Sending DMs without knowing why someone would buy.
>
> Then wonder why LinkedIn isn't generating clients.
>
> The goal isn't to be more active.
>
> It's to be more intentional.
>
> 5 conversations with the right buyers > 50 conversations with people who'll never buy.

## How it maps to Faizan
| Post says | For Faizan |
|---|---|
| Buyers | 1. People who can hire him in India (founders, hiring managers, recruiters at AI or automation companies). 2. Later: business owners who could buy his freelance AI automation services |
| 5 useful comments on relevant posts | 3 comments a day on posts by the right people (daily engagement list) |
| Follow up with qualified prospects | Up to 2 follow ups a day with people who replied to him |
| Note 1 real customer problem | 1 problem a day into `projects/linkedin-branding/engagement/problems.md` |
| Don't comment on random viral posts | No viral slot. Reach from the wrong audience does not count |
| Don't connect with every "CEO" | Connect only after a real exchange in a thread |
| 60 minutes a day | His budget is about 10 minutes a day; job search comes first |
