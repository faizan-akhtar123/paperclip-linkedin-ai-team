# Paperclip Usage Rules

- **Claude Pro plan limits:** keep the team small (3 agents to start), heartbeats infrequent (weekly or twice a week), and budgets low. Warn Faizan before anything that could use a lot of his quota.
- **No LinkedIn automation:** agents never post, comment, like, connect or message on LinkedIn. They only draft. Faizan does every LinkedIn action by hand.
- **Verify, don't guess:** Paperclip changes quickly. Check the official repo or docs before giving install or config steps, and update `references/paperclip/` with what you confirm.
- **Teach while doing:** when setting something up in Paperclip, explain in one or two lines what that concept is and why it matters.
