# Post 04: human review point

_Status: DRAFT (Writer agent LIN-3, hook fixed by Claude Code for accuracy, 2026-10-22)_

82 percent of executives say they can keep their AI agents in check. Only 5 percent have the controls to back it up.

That is from research OutSystems released with CIO Dive and KPMG on 16 October 2026, covering 858 executives. The 5 percent are the ones with automated controls, audit trails and formal human review in place.

I run a small AI team for my LinkedIn, built in Paperclip with Claude Code. Three agents: a Content Strategist, a Trend Researcher and a Writer. They research and draft. They never post, like or comment. I review everything and post by hand.

That review has already paid off.

The Trend Researcher's first note presented an untested project as if it were proven experience. It read well. It was wrong. I caught it at review, and it is now a team rule.

Nothing irreversible happened, because the agents only draft. The review point is the reason.

I did not bolt it on at the end as a safety feature. The whole setup hangs off it.

If you work with AI agents, where is your human review point, and what has it caught so far?

If you have any boring, repetitive task to automate, you can get in touch with me.
