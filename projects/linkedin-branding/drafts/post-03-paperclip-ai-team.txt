The first problem in my AI company was a space in my name.

For the last 5 days I have been setting up Paperclip, an open source tool that runs a team of AI agents like a small company. Every agent has a role, a manager and its own tasks. Claude Code is the brain behind each one.

My company is called LinkedIn Brand Studio. Its job is to help me with exactly this: writing here.

Where it stands today:

Two of three agents are hired. A Content Strategist plans the week, and a Trend Researcher tracks what is changing in AI automation. A Writer comes next.

The Strategist already did its first real job. It gave me 3 post ideas, and two of them became my first two posts.

What broke:

The agents would not start. The test only said "probe failed". After a lot of digging, the cause was the space in my Windows username. One PATH fix and it worked.

Then I noticed a neck stretch reminder I had set for myself was running inside every agent run too, quietly using my Claude quota. Fixed that as well.

The rules I gave the team:

They never post, like or comment on LinkedIn. They research and draft. I review and post.

They read one file of real facts about me and are not allowed to invent numbers.

I am on a Claude Pro plan, so each agent gets a small limit per run and no automatic schedule yet.

Microsoft's Work Trend Index says every employee will become an "agent boss". My lesson so far: being the boss is less about prompts and more about setting limits and checking the work.

If you could hand one boring task to an AI teammate tomorrow, what would it be?
