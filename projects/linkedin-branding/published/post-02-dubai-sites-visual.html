The process on paper
vs the process on site

What 4 years of HVAC coordination in Dubai taught me

ON PAPER
Drawing issued
↓
Team builds it
↓
Inspection
↓
Done
ON SITE
Drawing revised
↓
Site still has the old one
↓
Duct clashes with pipe
↓← nobody’s fault

↓
Done, but late

what changed, who needs to know, by when.
